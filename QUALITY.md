# Grasp visual quality bar

Every generated world must pass all five checks. Score new
domains against this list; fix failures until all pass.

1. Seated parts: every solid touches another part or the ground.
   Only particles, light rays, and flames float free.
2. Framed context: whole assembly plus ground visible, nothing
   important cropped, no single slab filling the frame.
3. Material richness: the hero part carries texture (a real GLB
   mesh or visible grain); no pure black or white except space.
4. Readable spine: 3-8 beats, labels of 1-3 words, only the
   current beat's parts labeled, spine order matches the scene.
5. Correct-first: the world opens drill-free; breakage appears
   only on the drilled beat after an explicit fault drill.
